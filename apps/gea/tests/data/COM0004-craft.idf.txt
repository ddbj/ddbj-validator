Investigation Title	Spatial analysis of cellular interaction of colorectal tumors
Experiment Description	Accumulating evidence has determined the critical role of tumor stroma-infiltrating cells in the tumor microenvironment of advanced colorectal cancer (CRC). MSI-H CRCs are characterized by abundant T cells and responsive to immunotherapy. On the other hand, mesenchymal MSS CRCs enriched with myeloid cells and fibroblasts, contain few cytotoxic cells and exhibit immunotherapy resistance. Interestingly, both type of CRCs can arise from colon tumors with BRAF mutation. To elucidate the mechanisms underlying these distinct phenotypes, we analyzed spatial interactions among stromal cell populations, aiming to uncover how their crosstalk shapes the tumor microenvironment.
